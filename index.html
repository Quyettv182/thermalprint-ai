The problem: prints fail thermally, and slicers can't see it coming

Warping, weak layer adhesion, heat creep, and surface defects are usually thermal problems. Today's slicers slice geometry — they never simulate how heat builds up and dissipates layer by layer, so users only discover the failure after hours of printing and wasted filament.

Three modules, one goal: fewer failed prints

ThermalPrint AI works alongside the slicers people already use — Bambu Studio, OrcaSlicer, PrusaSlicer, and Cura.

01
Module 1 · Assess

Thermal risk simulation

ThermalPrint AI simulates how heat behaves during your print and visualizes the risk as an easy-to-read heatmap — so problem zones are visible before a single gram of filament is spent.

02
Module 2 · Repair

Automatic mesh repair

Common mesh defects are detected and fixed automatically, with a clear report of what was changed — and an honest warning when a model cannot be saved, instead of a silently broken print.

03
Module 3 · Enhance

Dynamic G-code optimization

Print parameters are adjusted dynamically to balance speed, part strength, and surface quality — tuned to the result you are after.
